###  The Key to Mass-Energy Conversion: Fixed-Chirality Quantum Twistor Coupled with Spatial Spinor Locking

## I. Clifford: The Insight That Matter Is Curvature of Space

In discussing the nature of mass-energy conversion, one cannot overlook **William Kingdon Clifford**. In 1876, forty years before Einstein published general relativity, Clifford made the following assertion in a short lecture titled "On the Space-Theory of Matter":

> "I hold that... matter and energy are merely different manifestations of the curvature of space."

In Clifford's view, matter is not some independent entity that "exists in" space, but rather a local geometric feature of space itself—just as mountains are not "on" the Earth's surface, but are undulations of the surface itself. He further speculated that the motion of matter is merely the propagation of space curvature, and that the equivalence of gravitational and inertial mass arises from their sharing a common geometric origin.

Clifford's insight, lacking mathematical tools and experimental support at the time, was widely regarded as philosophical speculation and set aside. Nevertheless, its intellectual lineage continued through Einstein's general relativity. Wheeler's "geometrodynamics" program is continuous with Clifford's assertion that "physics is geometry," seeking to reduce matter, charge, spin, and all physical quantities to the geometric configuration of space.

However, although general relativity geometrized gravity as spacetime curvature, it left behind an asymmetry: the source of curvature—matter—is still inserted into the right-hand side of the equation from the outside in the form of the **stress-energy tensor**. In the Einstein field equation G_μν = (8πG/c⁴) T_μν, T_μν represents the distribution of matter and energy; it tells spacetime how to curve. But the content of T_μν itself—what matter actually is—is not explained by the equation itself; it must be supplied from outside. Matter tells spacetime how to curve—but what is matter itself? General relativity is silent on this question. Clifford's program that "matter = space curvature" requires a concrete physical mechanism to realize it: how does space "curve into" matter? And how is this curved state maintained without springing back?

## II. From Gravity to Mass: Spatial Compression Must Be Locked

The SEQ model of ZOU (2025) begins with a simple and direct line of reasoning, attempting to bridge Clifford's program and contemporary physics.

The starting point is an observational fact: **gravity is the stretching of space**. General relativity has established that the spacetime around a mass source is stretched—the metric deviates from the Minkowski form, and space expands outward. Gravitational wave detection (from 2015 onward) has further confirmed the dynamic propagation of this stretching effect.

If gravity equals the stretching of space, then within the framework of geometric ontology, Clifford's insight leads us to a natural symmetry question:

> **If mass produces gravity—i.e., the stretching of space—then is mass itself the compression of space?**

The point of this reasoning is: in an elastic medium, local compression necessarily causes surrounding stretching; conversely, observed surrounding stretching necessarily implies a source of compression somewhere. If gravity is the stretching deformation of space, then the source of gravity—mass—should be the compression deformation of space. This inference provides a clear operational direction for the program that "matter = space curvature."

Furthermore, if mass is merely spatial compression, then **compression must be locked to be maintained**. A compressed spring, if unconstrained, immediately rebounds and releases energy. Similarly, if mass were merely transient spatial compression, it could not exist stably—it would immediately rebound into outward-propagating radiation. In reality, however, mass is stable (at least on certain timescales), implying that there must exist some kind of **locking mechanism** in space that can fix the compression deformation energy in a local region and prevent elastic rebound.

Thus, the role of the Higgs mechanism is reinterpreted: **the Higgs mechanism is very likely the field-theoretic representation of this locking mechanism**. It does not endow mass through some mysterious "Yukawa coupling," but rather stabilizes the compression deformation of space into a persistent mass state through a geometric locking operation.

## III. From the Chiral Selectivity of the Higgs Mechanism to the Geometric Analogy of the Locking Mechanism

The Higgs mechanism itself contains a key feature: **chiral selectivity**. In the Standard Model, the Higgs field couples via Yukawa interactions only with left-chiral fermions; right-chiral particles do not participate in this coupling. This chiral preference—the fact that the Higgs mechanism intrinsically distinguishes left from right—is an experimentally confirmed phenomenon.

From this observation, ZOU (2025) proposes: if the Higgs mechanism is a locking operation, then this locking operation must necessarily possess chiral selectivity. In other words, **it is precisely the chiral selectivity of the Higgs mechanism itself that suggests the locking mechanism should exhibit chiral characteristics analogous to a threaded screw cap**.

Accordingly, the author introduces an intuitive mechanical analogy to depict this chiral locking mechanism: **the screw cap and the ratchet**.

**The screw cap**: a threaded cap whose internal threads precisely match the external threads of the bottle mouth. When the cap is rotated clockwise, it is progressively "tightened" and eventually locked into place; reverse rotation immediately releases it. This mechanical system has two key features:

1. **Chiral preference**: locking can only be achieved with a specific rotation direction; the reverse direction cannot lock.
2. **Unidirectional locking**: once tightened into place, ordinary external forces cannot loosen it.

**The ratchet**: a combination of a gear with unidirectional teeth and a pawl. The ratchet allows the shaft to rotate in only one direction; reverse rotation is blocked by the pawl. Combining the ratchet with a torsion spring—a preloaded spring attempting to rotate the shaft in reverse, but continuously blocked by the ratchet—creates a system that can permanently store elastic potential energy.

Together, the screw cap and the ratchet depict a complete picture: **locking requires both chiral matching (the screw cap) and unidirectional anti-reversal (the ratchet)**. The Higgs mechanism already demonstrates chiral matching through its coupling exclusively to left-chiral fermions. What the Standard Model does not explain is what mechanism prevents the stored compression deformation energy from being released in reverse—this is precisely the part that the ratchet supplements.

## IV. Fixed Chiral Spin of Space as the Physical Basis for Chiral Locking

The screw-cap-and-ratchet analogy suggests: **space itself is a chiral locking medium**. This chirality originates from the fixed chiral ground-state spin of SEQ (Space Elementary Quanta)—each SEQ carries an immutable "internal thread direction," constituting the chiral background of all space. This hypothesis is consistent with the discovery of parity non-conservation proposed by Lee and Yang in 1956 and experimentally verified by Wu the following year: if space itself possesses fixed chirality, then the violation of mirror symmetry is not a special property of the weak interaction, but rather a natural manifestation of the structure of space itself.

When spatial compression deformation occurs, the deformation energy attempts to be stored. However, storage can succeed only when the following condition is met:

> **The chiral direction of the spatial twistor of the compression deformation must match the direction of the fixed chiral spin of SEQ.**

This is analogous to the internal threads of a screw cap needing to match the external threads of the bottle mouth in order to tighten. If the directions are opposite, locking fails—the compression deformation immediately rebounds elastically, releasing energy as radiation. This is precisely the reason why antimatter cannot exist stably: the rotation direction of its structural matrix is opposite to the fixed chirality of SEQ, just as a reverse-threaded cap attempting to screw onto a standard-threaded bottle mouth.

Once locked successfully, the compression deformation energy is stably stored, forming a **mass state**. This mass state does not spontaneously rebound because the "ratchet" mechanism—the coupling between the fixed chiral spin of SEQ and the deformation twistor—continuously prevents reverse release. Only when sufficient external energy (such as in high-energy collisions) overcomes the locking barrier does the mass state "unlock," releasing the compression energy as radiation or other particles.

## V. Coupling of Two Types of Spatial Spinors

The geometric core of this locking mechanism lies in **the coupling of two types of spatial spinors**.

The first is the **Kerr spinor**, originating from the spacetime torsion generated by rotating mass in general relativity. When a massive body rotates, it drags the surrounding spacetime into rotation—the "frame-dragging" effect, most prominent near Kerr black holes. The Kerr spinor describes this macroscopic spatial torsional deformation.

The second is the **Einstein-Cartan torsion**, which arises from introducing spin-torsion into gravitational theory, describing the contribution of matter's spin density to spacetime torsion. In the SEQ model, the Einstein-Cartan torsion corresponds to the coupling between SEQ spin and the elastic bonds between adjacent quanta at the sub-Planckian scale—the microscopic "spinorial degrees of freedom" of space itself.

The coupling of the two constitutes the complete geometric picture of the locking mechanism: the Kerr spinor provides the macroscopic force that "tightens" space, while the Einstein-Cartan torsion provides the microscopic ratchet that "locks" the tightened state. Both are indispensable—tightening without locking results in immediate rebound and release of deformation energy as radiation; locking without tightening means there is no energy to be stored. Stable locking can only form when the chiral directions of both are aligned.

The Higgs mechanism is reinterpreted in this picture: it is not an independent fundamental field, but rather the quantum field-theoretic equivalent of the cooperative locking effect of the Kerr spinor and the Einstein-Cartan torsion. The so-called "Mexican hat potential" of the Higgs is merely the mathematical expression of the stability surface of spatial deformation energy under chiral locking conditions. The Higgs boson is the resonant excitation of this locking mechanism at a specific energy scale.

## VI. Unifying Explanatory Power of the Locking Mechanism

Once this "fixed-chirality quantum twistor coupled with spatial spinor locking" mechanism is established, it can unify multiple phenomena in a concise manner:

**Mass generation**: The mass of ordinary matter (baryons) originates from the synergy between QCD (SU(3)) compression of space and chiral spinor locking—the strong interaction provides the compressive force, while the fixed chiral background provides the locking condition. Their combination produces what we call "mass"—stable spatial compression energy.

**Matter-antimatter asymmetry**: The structural matrix rotation of positive particles (such as electrons) matches the fixed chiral spin of SEQ, allowing stable locking. The structural matrix rotation of antiparticles (such as positrons) is opposite and cannot lock effectively—they either decay rapidly after formation or require additional energy compensation during formation, placing them at a natural disadvantage in the early universe. This explains why the universe today is composed almost entirely of matter rather than antimatter. ZOU (2025) further predicts that the magnetic moments of the positron and electron should differ by a small but measurable amount, a prediction currently being tested by the precision measurement experiment of the Fan-Myers-Sukra-Gabrielse collaboration.

**Dark matter**: Under the lower compression conditions of the later universe, locking can still occur but with insufficient strength—forming "weakly locked" compression states. These states produce gravity (because locked compression still stretches surrounding space), but produce no electromagnetic effects (because the locking strength does not reach the threshold for forming charge structures), and are extremely difficult to unlock. This is consistent with the observational characteristics of dark matter.

**Black holes**: Under extreme conditions, locking reaches saturation—all storable compression deformation energy is locked, and the interior of the event horizon forms a "fully locked state." The mass of a black hole is the maximum locking density of spatial deformation energy, and its horizon is the locking-unlocking boundary. Since there is an upper limit to the elastic tension between SEQ, singularities do not form.

## VII. Conclusion

From the above discussion, a relatively complete picture of mass-energy conversion emerges:

Clifford foresaw the geometrization program that "matter = space curvature" a century and a half ago, but was limited by his era and could not provide a concrete mechanism. ZOU (2025), starting from the observational fact that "gravity = stretching of space," derives that "mass = compression of space," and further argues that compression must be locked to remain stable. From the chiral selectivity of the Higgs mechanism itself—coupling only with left-chiral fermions—the author derives that the locking mechanism must necessarily possess chiral selectivity, and makes it concrete through the mechanical analogy of the screw cap and ratchet. Combined with the indication of spatial chirality from parity non-conservation experiments, the locking mechanism is identified as chiral spinor locking in a fixed-chirality spatial background—the coupling of the Kerr spinor and the Einstein-Cartan torsion, with its chiral selectivity originating from the inherent chirality of the SEQ ground-state spin. This mechanism uniformly explains mass generation, the nature of the Higgs mechanism, matter-antimatter asymmetry, dark matter formation, and black hole maintenance.

In the final picture, the key to mass-energy conversion is: **whether the spatial compression deformation energy can be locked by the coupling of fixed-chirality quantum twistor and spatial spinor**. Locked is mass (matter), unlocked is energy (radiation), weakly locked is dark matter, and fully locked to saturation is a black hole. The transitions among these four states are uniformly regulated by a single mechanism.

Whether this picture represents the final truth of physical reality awaits experimental testing—especially the precision measurement of the difference between positron and electron magnetic moments, which could serve as a key validation of the model. However, at minimum, it provides a self-consistent, geometric, and unified theoretical framework for mass-energy conversion.
